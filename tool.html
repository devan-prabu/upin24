How do I prepare my template? (one-time, 2 minutes)
Open your existing Excel form and type a marker like {{wir_no}}, {{date}}, {{zone}}, {{contractor}} into each cell you edit every time. Save it once — that's your master template forever. WIRflow finds the markers automatically and replaces only them, so every logo, border, merged cell, and formula stays exactly as it was.

No template handy? Download a sample WIR template to try the flow right now.

2 Tell it what changed — in plain English

Separate items with commas or new lines. "today", "tomorrow", "next monday" become real dates. Ctrl+Enter also applies.

3 Generate the filled package

Use any field as a token — e.g. WIR_{{wir_no}}_{{zone}}_{{date}}
