FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do consultants or clients need to use WIRflow?+
No. WIRflow lives entirely on your side of the fence. The consultant never logs in, never trains on it, and never approves its existence. The output is a perfect package delivered through whatever channel your project already uses.
What happens after I submit a package?+
When the consultant responds outside the system, you tap Approved, Rejected, or Resubmit — optionally with a photo of the signed copy. A resubmission automatically creates a linked revision.
Does WIRflow replace our document control system?+
No — it feeds it. WIRflow generates the perfect, audit-ready package; your document controller's platform stays the official record channel.
What if I have no signal on site?+
Offline drafts are a top fast-follower priority — the form works with no signal, and the package generates the moment connectivity returns.
Who owns the data?+
You do. A full export of your register and every package as a ZIP is available at any time — a deliberate answer to enterprise lock-in.
Is my company's WIR template supported?+
Yes — template upload with merge-field mapping is a day-one capability, not a customization service. A new project is usable in under a working day, self-service.
